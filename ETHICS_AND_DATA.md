# Ethical and data-release boundaries

- The 40 customer-service cases are labelled as fictional and were synthetically authored; no real customer interview or recording is included. Synthetic dialogue can nonetheless incidentally resemble real identifiers. Authors must inspect the examples prior to publication.
- The files under `pilot_inputs/` contain **assessor-visible evidence only**, not confidential causal/reference classifications. Do not join these inputs with hidden design keys in a public directory.
- Public `frozen_pilot_outputs/` contain model-generated assessments, including model response IDs; the authors should review their suitability for public release.
- Private C3 rating worksheets, adjudication records and reference keys are not included.
- The original synthetic reference key was constructed in scenario design; it was not independently validated. Any new benchmark will need independent audits of underlying cause and condition-specific evidential warrant.
- No actual managers, employees or organisational outcomes were evaluated. Proposed real-data field work requires data permission and ethical review.
